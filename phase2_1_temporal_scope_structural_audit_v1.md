## 2. Grammar consistency

PASS.

`phase2_1_protocol_v3.md` explicitly removes candidate predicates `BEFORE(Action,Action)` and `AFTER(Action,Action)`.

`phase2_1_serl_grammar_v2.json` contains no `BEFORE` or `AFTER` predicate and retains the other frozen relational predicates and grammar restrictions.

The earlier `phase2_1_serl_grammar_v1.json` remains a historical/provenance artifact; it is not the prospective execution grammar after v3.

## 3. Candidate enumeration consistency

PASS at the scope level.

The production and independent reference closure surfaces no longer admit `BEFORE` or `AFTER` in their predicate arity maps. Therefore candidates generated by those surfaces cannot contain either temporal predicate.

No replacement temporal feature, hidden timestamp feature, temporal tie-breaker, or benchmark-specific shortcut was introduced.

The pre-existing broader candidate-enumeration completeness issue is intentionally not solved by this target; it remains a separate final-conformance concern.

## 4. K_A consistency

PASS at the scope level.

K_A is restricted to rules expressible in the amended grammar. A rule requiring `BEFORE` or `AFTER` is therefore inadmissible prospectively.

The existing acquisition requirements are otherwise unchanged. No A-derived temporal state is substituted for the removed predicates.

## 5. Retrieval consistency

PASS at the scope level.

Retrieval operates over the amended candidate language. The removed temporal role-to-Fact selector is no longer a required Phase 2.1 retrieval dependency.

Observable Facts may still carry event-order provenance in existing infrastructure, but no candidate-level temporal truth function consumes that provenance.

## 6. Prediction consistency

PASS.

The prospective consequence domain remains exactly:

`ENABLES(target)`

`NONENABLES(contrast,target)`

The prediction must still precede the decisive intervention. This ordering is an execution/attribution requirement and is not removed by killing candidate temporal operators.

## 7. Attribution consistency

The attribution chain and precedence remain unchanged. Chronological ordering of actual retrieval, prediction, intervention, consequence, verification, and counterfactual events remains available to the scientific attribution procedure.

## 8. Leakage consistency
