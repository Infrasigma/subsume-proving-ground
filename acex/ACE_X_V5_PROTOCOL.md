# ACE-X V5 Scientific Protocol

## Claim boundary

V5 is a model-independent cognitive substrate experiment. It is **not** an ASI claim.

V5 changes the object of learning from task solutions alone to the cognitive mechanisms that decide
what to retrieve, what experiment to run, how to search, and what mechanism revision to promote.

External learned models may propose hypotheses, but they cannot define truth, hidden evaluation,
promotion, or rollback.

## Frozen capabilities

V5-A  Open-world grounding: raw object/relation observations become ID-independent relational state.

V5-B  Active causal inquiry: competing predictive hypotheses induce discriminating interventions;
       observed outcomes update the surviving hypothesis set.

V5-C  Surprise-driven memory: prediction errors and failures affect future retrieval priority.

V5-D  Semantic abstraction: equivalent structural descriptions are compared through normalized
       representations rather than raw identifier equality.

V5-E  Mechanism search: the runtime can evaluate alternative cognitive strategies, not only
       alternative task concepts.

V5-F  Independent successor evaluation: mechanism promotion is decided on evaluator-owned tasks
       whose hidden targets are not constructed from the candidate's learned library.

V5-G  Cross-surface transfer: successor tasks vary identifiers, topology, noise and task surface.

V5-H  Proof-carrying promotion: promotion requires lower resource-normalized future discovery
       cost on every hidden successor block, no verified regression, lineage, and rollback.

V5-I  Endogenous curriculum: unresolved verified failures produce the next test objective.

V5-J  Continual knowledge: verified abstractions survive deletion of raw episodes and are reused
       on later tasks.

V5-K  Mechanism-level self-improvement: the promoted object must be a change to search/retrieval/
       inquiry strategy, not merely an additional task-specific concept.

## Terminal acceptance

The protected terminal must pass three evaluator-owned seed blocks.

Each block must exercise:
1. grounding invariance,
2. active intervention,
3. surprise-linked memory retrieval,
4. mechanism selection on independent successor tasks,
5. cross-surface transfer,
6. knowledge persistence after raw-episode deletion,
7. curriculum generation after a forced bottleneck,
8. promotion plus rollback.

For mechanism promotion:

R = C(T | K_after) / C(T | K_before)

Promotion requires R < 0.80 for every independently generated successor task,
and the candidate may not regress on any previously accepted capability.

## Kill rule

A scientific failure after mechanical defects are excluded kills V5.

Thresholds, hidden seeds, evaluator logic, task construction, and acceptance rules
must not be weakened after observing results.

## What V5 still does not establish

Even a V5 PASS would not establish AGI or ASI. A subsequent gate must require:
- substantially broader open-world task generation,
- learned rather than hand-authored cognitive mechanisms,
- causal/world-model learning in environments with persistent state,
- novel tool and representation invention,
- mechanism transfer across independently implemented domains,
- and repeated mechanism-level improvement without task-family-specific adapters.
