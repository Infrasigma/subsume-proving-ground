# ACE-X Integrated Research Protocol

ACE-X is a model-independent cognitive substrate. External learned models are optional implementation components only; they cannot decide acceptance, hidden-task generation, or truth.

Core requirements:
- verified knowledge acquisition
- representation invention and revision
- prediction and intervention testing
- active experiment selection
- compositional search and planning
- failure diagnosis and strategy change
- self-generated capability challenges
- verified improvement of the search/representation machinery
- cross-domain transfer
- persistent knowledge with deletion/recovery
- resource-normalized future-cost reduction

Frozen gates:
G1 verified abstraction retention and raw-episode deletion control.
G2 autonomous representation revision with hidden transfer.
G3 correct selection of informative interventions between competing hypotheses.
G4 hidden compositional transfer with at least 0.90 accuracy and >=25% cost reduction versus fresh search.
G5 forced bottleneck followed by autonomous generic strategy change and hidden success.
G6 self-generated next challenge from the measured capability frontier.
G7 independently verified mechanism improvement that reduces normalized future discovery cost across three successor tasks without regressions.
G8 transfer across three independently implemented surfaces with renamed symbols/topology changes and no family adapters.
G9 pure-substrate model ablation: the core must pass without external learned models.
G10 resource-normalized compounding: successor acquisition cost must fall relative to a fresh learner.

Two deterministic hidden seed blocks are mandatory.

Terminal:
PASS only when every gate passes on both blocks.
KILL when any gate fails on either block after mechanical defects are excluded.

No threshold, hidden seed, evaluator, or task-family-specific mechanism may be changed after observing scientific results.

Optional model interfaces are replaceable components only. The evaluator remains model-independent.

CI-trigger revision: integrated gate unchanged.

Clean-run synchronization marker.
